# Reference: "Bigger Box for arduino and a standard arcade button"

Sheldon McGee, printables.com/model/871092 (a remix of Niklas's "Buzzer / big red button"), CC BY 4.0.
Downloaded by Garett on 2026-10-05 as a starting point for the next button: a bought 30 mm arcade
button under a big printed dome, the electronics in a box below.

What the three parts are (measured from the STLs, see the section drawings in the shell README's
"big dome" section):

- `big-red-button-bottom-v3.stl`: an 85 × 85 × 53.5 mm open box, 2 mm walls, a lip for the top.
- `big-red-button-top-v3.stl`: the 85 mm lid with a round collar (Ø76, 12.6 mm tall) carrying a
  25 mm square pocket for the arcade button's body and three Ø6 holes on a 44 mm circle.
- `big-red-button-button-v3.stl`: the dome, Ø91, 40 mm from rim to crown, 2 mm wall, closed by a
  flat floor 2 mm up from the rim. Three Ø6 hollow legs, 14 mm long, stand down from the floor into
  the collar's holes: they are the guide, and the floor itself lands on the arcade button's cap.
  The file is stored crown-down.

Why it matters to us: the dome is the moving part and the arcade button is the spring, the click and
the stop, so the printed mechanism is just a guide. Ours puts the hex inside a transparent dome
(the floor becomes a carrier) and the Atom, Rider and cell in the box.
