What all three share

No stack. The cap, cage, peg, collar and flexures are gone. Foam tape between the Atom's panel and what presses it is the give; something solid (the top, or the Atom's own rim) is the stop. Nothing has to be printed to a tenth of a millimetre, and tape under the Atom no longer matters.

Built right side up. Lid, cell and Rider, platform, Atom and hex, cables, lantern; the housing goes on last, like a hat. Everything is in the open until the end.

The light is a lantern. A white cup over the hex, standing proud of the deck, glowing from its sides as well as its face; the LEDs sit 10 mm or more under its face, twice what the dots need to blend. A well round the hex keeps the light out of the rest of the box.

Looks like the rest of it. Cut corners, not rounded; a waist line at the platform; one groove on the deck, round where you press. Two filaments: black for everything, transparent for the lantern. The light is the only colour on it.
